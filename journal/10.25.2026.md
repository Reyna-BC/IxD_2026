## My Thoughts
Continuing from part 1 on chapter 4 when it comes to the different types of constrictions, in the rest of the chapter we talk about how do people would be able to approach these designs. Setting up a goal is the main point of making a design of any kind. The following steps are to now
the execution; planning how the user's actions and being able to assume how they would interact with said design. Then how the user would solve or know how to use it. The rest of the steps are the response to these choices, letting the users know or receiving feedback to what they chose to approach with.
Whether it is wrong or right, from there the user will see what is the next steps to meeting that goal. If the outcome/feedback was incorrect, they approach the design in another way. If it is correct, the user will see progress or reach their goal. The main point of this system is that you want to limit the least amount of 
trial and errors, or at least make the approach to the goal more subtle to understand. Otherwise, you leave the users feeling a certain way on unable to meet the goal and not continue.
